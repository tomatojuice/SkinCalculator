# 🎨 Skin Calculator

English | [日本語](README_ja.md)

A stylish, multi-functional calculator app that lets you "dress up" the UI to suit your taste.
Make your daily calculations more fun and convenient.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **🎨 Various Themes:** Easily switch between Dark, Light, Pink, and other skins.
- **💰 Tax Calculation:** Customize tax rates and calculate Tax-included/excluded prices with one tap.
- **📝 History Log:** Check your past calculations anytime in the history list.
- **🌐 Bilingual Support:** Fully supports both English and Japanese.
- **📳 Haptic Feedback:** Toggle vibration on/off for a better typing experience.
